Lagrange multipliers are used to find the maximum of a function f(x) subject to the constraint g(x) = 0

## The Method
1) Define a new objective function called the **Lagrangian** 
$$L(x,\lambda) = f(x) - \lambda g(x)$$
2) Find the stationary points of L with respect to both $\mathbf{x}$ and $\lambda$, i.e solve the system of equations $\nabla f(\mathbf{x} = \lambda \nabla g(\mathbf{x} )$ and $g(\mathbf{x})=0$ for both unknowns.
3) Then the constrained extrema are found among the solutions to these equations. Identify their nature.
